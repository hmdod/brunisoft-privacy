Operator and contact

Video Converter BruniSoft is provided by Bruno under the BruniSoft brand. For privacy questions, requests or support, contact brukshields02@gmail.com. This policy applies to the Android application with package com.brunisoft.videoconverter.

Videos and files

You select videos using the Android file picker. The app reads the selected file and metadata needed to display options and convert it, such as its name, size, duration, dimensions and codecs. Processing takes place on your device. The app does not upload your videos to the operator or AdMob. If you choose cloud documents or destinations, your selected provider may transfer those files over the internet under its own policies.

Storage and permissions

The app uses access granted to selected documents without requesting broad gallery, contacts, location or microphone access. Android may retain the document read grant until it is revoked or the app is uninstalled. Conversion uses a private temporary file. The app attempts to delete it on completion, cancellation or failure; files left after an unexpected shutdown are removed on the next conversion or can be removed by clearing the app cache. Removing an incomplete destination depends on the provider permissions. Saved videos remain under your control and may remain after uninstalling the app.

Preferences and backup

The app stores your theme preference and whether it has already shown the notification permission request locally. App backup rules include only those preferences, which Android may copy through its backup or device-transfer service according to device settings. These rules do not include conversion videos or ad consent records. Notifications and the foreground service let you monitor and cancel conversion while using other apps.

Advertising and third parties

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the User Messaging Platform (UMP). Test versions use demonstration ads that do not generate revenue. Advertising and consent services can communicate with Google even in test versions. Depending on configuration and privacy choices, these services may process IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app/ad interactions, diagnostic information and device/app identifiers. The app removes the Android Advertising ID permission and does not access device location APIs. This does not eliminate other data processed by Google services. Google may use this data for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention under its policies. The ads SDK encrypts network communications in transit using TLS.

Privacy choices

Where applicable, the app checks UMP and displays messages configured in AdMob before requesting ads. Use Ad privacy in app settings to review or change options available in your region. Choices may allow limited ads under Google rules. If authorization to request ads cannot be established, conversion remains available without loading the banner. You can also use Android privacy and advertising controls.

Retention and deletion

The operator does not maintain a database of users' videos or accounts. The app has no registration or login. You can delete videos in the destination you chose, clear the app cache or data, or uninstall the app. Clearing app data removes local preferences and may require new consent choices. Data already processed by Google follows the retention and deletion mechanisms in Google policies; we do not promise a retention period we do not control. Contact Bruno about processing under the operator's responsibility. Do not email personal videos or documents unless needed for your request.

Audience and requests

The app is a video conversion tool and does not request user names, ages or registration. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy question about use of the app, use the contact above.

Changes

This policy may change as the app or its services change. The update date appears on this page. Changes requiring new privacy choices will be handled through the applicable in-app flow.
